About the team

Coordinate Robotics is a student team learning engineering by building a real competition robot — together, on a deadline, with a machine that has to work.

Who we are

We are students who wanted a team that treats the robot as an engineering product: specified, documented, built, tested, and improved in public.

The name is the working standard. Mechanisms, wiring, software, and match decisions should agree with one another — precision in every axis.

Mission

Compete honestly, learn the full stack of a robotics program, and represent our school with professional conduct.

  • Field a reliable robot each season
  • Train mechanical, electrical, and software skills
  • Communicate clearly with mentors and sponsors

Engineering

Structure, drivetrain, and manufacturing. We design in CAD, prototype the risky joints, and measure what we change.

Programming

Control, autonomous routines, and diagnostics. Software is a subsystem of the robot, maintained with the same care as hardware.

Design

Packaging, service access, and how the robot is presented in the pit. A machine that is easy to work on stays in more matches.

What we learn

  • Mechanical design and fabrication
  • Embedded software and controls
  • Electrical layout and safety
  • Planning against a hard deadline
  • Technical writing and sponsor communication
  • Match strategy and scouting
